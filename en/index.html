存目 (cunmu) is the classical Chinese catalogue term for a book recorded by its title alone.

Keep the titles.They're enough to remember by.

The anime you stayed up all night to finish one summer. The novel you read on the train to work. The save file with 200 hours on it. Cunmu gathers what you've logged across the web into a single book, and the book is about you.

You've been keeping notes for years

A tick on Bangumi, a “read” on Douban, a play counter on Steam quietly adding up. Hundreds of entries, maybe thousands, spread over three or four sites, each one a single line.

Put together, they are the story of how you got here: the summer you fell for science fiction, the year you read on every commute, the show that kept you up until the sky went light.

Favourites, lasting interests, the named chapters of your life, your own notes. Suggested from your records; one tap to keep or let go.

Bring it all in

A username, a backup file, a pasted note.

  • BangumiA username reads your public collection; a personal token reads the private one too. Once connected, it stays in sync.
  • DoubanAn Excel backup exported with 豆伴 or 豆备. Cunmu reads the file and never connects to Douban.
  • WeReadPaste your notes.
  • SteamYour games and the hours you played, with your own Web API key.

只存其目Keep the titles.

Only the title

The name comes from the Siku Quanshu, the vast imperial library compiled in 18th-century China. Some books were not copied in full; the editors kept only the title and a short abstract. That was called 存目 (cunmu), “keeping the title”.

We think that's enough. No essays, no star ratings. A name, a date, a line of your own. Years later you see it again, and the summer comes back with it.

Light and dark follow your system.

Privacy · 设置 › 隐私说明

Yours alone

No account. No Cunmu server. No analytics.

  1. Your records stay on your device. Cunmu only goes online to reach Bangumi or Steam, and only once you connect them.

  2. The Bangumi token and the Steam key are stored only on this device and are never written into an export.

  3. Export everything as one ZIP of Markdown, JSON and your own images. Restore it from the file, or delete it all.

  4. Douban is read from the backup file you choose, WeRead from the notes you paste. Cunmu never signs in to either.

The full details are in the app, under 设置 › 隐私说明 (Settings › Privacy).

Q&A

New phone?
Export a ZIP on the old one and restore it on the new one.
Is it affiliated with Bangumi or Douban?
No. It only reads your own records and never writes anything back.
Do I have to rate things?
No. Cunmu never asks for a score. A name, a date and a line of your own are enough.
Is the app in English?
Not yet. The interface is in Chinese only. Cunmu is built for people whose records live on Chinese services (Bangumi, Douban, WeRead), plus Steam.
Is there an iOS version?
Not yet. Cunmu runs on Android 7.0 or later and on Windows 10 / 11.
What about the covers in the screenshots?
They come from Bangumi and other sources and belong to their rights holders; they're shown only to illustrate the interface. The library is a sample.
